Velor Bay is a top-down 2D open-world street story game built for [make.fun](https://make.fun)'s "Make GTA 6" bounty ($1,666 in USDC). Explore a procedural five-district city as Kai Mercer: drive and steal cars, outrun the police under a 0–5 wanted system, fight rival gangs, and complete six chained story missions — from a driving tutorial to the final bank score. Vanilla JavaScript + Canvas, zero libraries, zero network requests, procedural art and synthesized WebAudio sound.

## Play

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

Controls: WASD / arrow keys to move and drive, E to enter or steal a car, mouse to aim, click to shoot, P to pause. Progress saves to localStorage.
